🌐 Internet Basics

Discover the world of the internet! Learn about websites, browsers, search engines, email and how to stay safe online.

🌍 What is the Internet?

The internet is a worldwide network that connects computers, mobile phones and other devices so they can share information.

We use the internet to learn new things, watch educational videos, communicate with people and find information.

Example: Students can use the internet to search for information about science, mathematics, history and other subjects.

💡 Uses of the Internet

The internet helps us in many different areas of our daily lives.

📚

Online Education

Learn new topics, read e-books and watch educational videos.

📧

Communication

Send emails, messages and communicate with others online.

🔍

Finding Information

Search for information about different topics and subjects.

🎥

Watching Videos

Watch educational videos, tutorials and documentaries.

💼

Online Services

Access useful public services, forms and other online resources.

🗺️

Maps & Directions

Find places, explore maps and understand directions.

🌐 What is a Web Browser?

A web browser is a software application that helps us open and view websites on the internet.

🌐

Google Chrome

A popular browser used to visit websites.

🦊

Mozilla Firefox

A web browser for accessing websites.

🌊

Microsoft Edge

A browser developed by Microsoft.

🧭

Safari

A web browser commonly used on Apple devices.

Remember: A web browser is an application used to visit websites. A search engine is a service used to find information online.

🔎 What is a Search Engine?

A search engine helps us find information on the internet by entering words or questions into a search box.

🔍

Google

Helps people find websites, images, videos and information.

🅱️

Bing

A search engine that helps users discover information.

🦆

DuckDuckGo

A search engine with a focus on user privacy.

🧑‍💻 How to Search on the Internet

Follow these simple steps to search for information online.

💻

Step 1

Open a web browser on your device.

🔍

Step 2

Open a search engine or use its search bar.

⌨️

Step 3

Type the topic you want to learn.

📖

Step 4

Review the results and open a useful website.

📧 What is Email?

Email stands for Electronic Mail. It is a way to send and receive digital messages over the internet.

Students can use email to communicate with teachers, receive school announcements and share documents.

Example: A student can send an email to a teacher to ask a question about a school assignment.

🛡️ Stay Safe on the Internet

The internet is useful for learning, but it is important to protect your personal information.

🔐

Keep Passwords Private

Never share your password with strangers. Ask a trusted adult for help when needed.

🚫

Avoid Unknown Links

Do not click suspicious links or download unknown files.

👤

Protect Personal Details

Do not share your address, phone number or school details publicly.

🧑‍🏫

Ask a Trusted Adult

Tell a parent or teacher if you see something confusing or uncomfortable.
